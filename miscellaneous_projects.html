Final Project for ENG ***

Probably the earliest project I completed was for my ENG *** class. I took this class my freshman year in the Fall of 2020 at WIU, where I learned SolidWorks or 3D CAD design. At the time, I had a Google Chromebook for a laptop, which was sufficient for my uses in High School but nowhere near capable enough to run SolidWorks (Dr. Shin probably had a good laugh after I asked him if I could get SolidWorks on it). We also had a "family" laptop, but this was also not powerful enough (Might have actually been worse than the Chromebook). Because of this, my parents got me a $200 refurbished HP Elitedesk for school work, which I still have and use to this day (Yes, I need to upgrade). The final project for this class was to select an item and reverse engineer the object in SolidWorks.
