Projects

Thesis Project

My research at the University of Georgia led me to develop a reinforcement learning framework for real-world systems. I applied this framework to a KUKA LBR iisy collaborative robot with an Ubiros soft gripper, teaching it to sort recyclables.

Senior Design Project

During my final year at Western Illinois University, I worked on a team that built and developed an autonomous vehicle from the ground up. I worked on the Navigation team, developing algorithms for perception, localization, and planning.
