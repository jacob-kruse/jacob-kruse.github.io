Senior Design Project

Introduction

At Western Illinois University, I obtained my Bachelor of Science degree in Electrical Engineering. As with most engineering programs, the final degree requirement is completing a senior design project (capstone). At the beginning of my senior year at WIU, we were assigned the task of designing, building, and developing an autonomous vehicle to participate in the Intelligent Ground Vehicle Competition (IGVC) in Michigan. My entire engineering senior class was divided into two teams for this project. The professors explained that each team were to design and build their own independent sytem, and at the end of the year, they would choose which vehicle would attend the competition. My team consisted of nine other students (see Figure 1), and we were subdivided further into the Mechanical team and Navigation team. The Mechanical team primarily worked on the physical aspects of the vehicle, including structure, components, and mobility. The Navigation team instead developed the autonomy of the vehicle, including the sensors, software, and communication. I served as the lead for the Navigation team, focusing on the localization system for the vehicle. After a year of hard work and continuos improvement to our system, we ended up producing a highly effective autonomous vehicle.
