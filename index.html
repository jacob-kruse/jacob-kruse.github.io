Jacob Kruse

I am currently working as a Robotics Engineer at L3Harris, working on manipulation algorithms and the deployment of these to the maritime robotics systems. I recently graduated from the University of Georgia with a Master of Science degree in Computer Science. During my time at UGA, I worked as a Graduate Research Assistant in the THINC Lab, researching reinforcement learning and its application to physical robotic systems. I also have a Bachelor of Science degree in Electrical Engineering from Western Illinois University. My expertise is heavily grounded in the multi-disciplinary field of robotics, which is one of my passions and an area in which I am constantly learning something new and exciting!
